What you get

A garden that looks after itself.

Regular garden maintenance is the cheapest insurance you can buy for your property. Caught early, weeds, pests and overgrowth cost a fraction to deal with. Left for a year, they cost a clean-up.

Our plans are not one-size-fits-all. Some properties need a weekly mow and quick tidy. Others want fortnightly with a bigger seasonal blitz. We tune the plan to your section, then stick to the schedule.

Common questions.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. Maintenance plans are month-to-month for residential. Commercial contracts can be longer if that suits your governance, but we never lock people in.

What if I need extra work between visits?

Just text Jeff. Most ad-hoc work gets slotted into the next routed visit, or sooner if it is urgent. Rates are transparent and on your contract.

Do you supply mulch and fertiliser?

Yes. We charge at trade rates and itemise on your invoice. You always know what you are paying for.

What about wet weather?

If we cannot mow safely we will message you and reschedule. Other tasks like weeding or pruning often go ahead in light rain depending on the day.

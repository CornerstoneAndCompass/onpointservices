Frequently asked.

If your question is not here, just ring Jeff. Easier than typing.

Do you do one-off jobs or only ongoing plans?

Both. Plenty of clients book a one-off clean up and never see us again, plenty go on a fortnightly maintenance plan, and plenty start with a build and roll into ongoing maintenance afterwards. Whatever fits.

What areas do you cover?

We service the greater Auckland region. From the North Shore down to Manukau, west to Henderson and east to Howick. If your property is in Auckland, we cover it.

How fast can you start?

For maintenance and small jobs, usually within the same week. Construction projects depend on scope and design phase, but we will give you a clear timeline at quote stage.

Are you insured and qualified?

Yes. Public liability, vehicle insurance, and trade qualifications across the crew. Happy to send our certificates of currency on request.

Do you supply plants and materials?

Yes. We have trade accounts with most NZ nurseries and suppliers, which means better plants, better prices and a single invoice for the whole job.

Will I always deal with the same person?

Jeff handles every quote. Crews are consistent on maintenance routes so the same people show up to your property each visit wherever possible.

Not surewhat you need?

That is what the free site walk is for. We take a look, talk options, and give you an honest answer.

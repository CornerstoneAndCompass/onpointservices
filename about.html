The story behind the badge.

On Point Services Ltd was started for a simple reason. Too many landscaping outfits ghost their clients, fudge their quotes and leave the place worse than they found it. We do the opposite, and we put it on the truck.

Who runs it

Jeff, on the tools, on the phone, on point.

Jeff started On Point after a decade running crews on commercial sites and residential builds. The company exists because clients kept asking for one number to call when their grounds needed sorting end-to-end.

That ethos still runs the place. When you ring 021 CALL JEFF, you get Jeff. When the crew arrives, they know what's been agreed because Jeff was there for the quote. No handoff, no telephone game, no surprises.

We are landscapers, but more than that we are property people. We treat every site like we own it.

Kiwi family-owned

Family-owned, NZ-based, in it for the long haul.

On Point is a Kiwi family business. Jeff is on the phone, the rest of the family is in the books, the yard, and the trucks. We are not a franchise, not a national chain, and not a fly-in operator chasing the next region.

That matters because the people who own this business are the same people you talk to. Decisions get made in one conversation, not three meetings. Mistakes get owned, not blamed on head office. And the work has to last, because we live here too.

When you support On Point, your money stays in NZ. We hire local crews, buy from local suppliers, and pay our taxes here. That is worth something.

The four things we don't compromise on.

Every business has a list. These are ours, and we will turn down work to keep them.

01

Show up

If we book a Tuesday, we are there Tuesday. Weather, traffic, hangovers. None of those are your problem. If something genuinely changes, you get a call before 7am, not a no-show at 9.

02

Quote what we mean

The number on the quote is the number on the invoice. If the scope changes mid-job we tell you, in writing, before we touch anything. No vague "allowances" that conveniently get used up.

03

Leave it tidy

Site leaves cleaner than we found it. Gear off the path, clippings removed, gates closed, dogs accounted for. Every visit, no exceptions.

04

Stand behind it

Plants we install, work we build. If something we did fails inside the warranty, we come back and put it right. No excuses, no fine print.

Built around three pillars.

Design, build, maintain. We do all three under one roof so there is no finger-pointing when something needs to change.

DESIGN

Concept, plant schedule, hardscape detail. We design for how your property will actually be used five years from now, not five minutes.

BUILD

Decks, walls, paving, planting, irrigation. The crew, the gear and the experience to deliver a project from machine work to final tidy.

MAINTAIN

Regular plans tuned to the property. Lawns mowed, beds weeded, hedges shaped, everything reported so you know what we did and what's next.

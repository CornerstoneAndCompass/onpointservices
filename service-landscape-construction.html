What we build.

In-house capability across the trades that make up a finished outdoor space. Less subcontracting, fewer handoffs, one accountable team.

01

Retaining Walls

Timber, block, gabion, concrete. Engineered when required, consented when required, built to last.

02

Decks & Pergolas

Hardwood, composite, kwila and pine. Pergolas, screens, privacy structures. Designed for NZ weather.

03

Paving & Paths

Natural stone, exposed aggregate, pavers, concrete. Laid on properly graded base courses, not just sand and hope.

04

Fencing & Gates

Boundary fences, privacy screens, automated gates, pool fencing. Compliant, square, plumb.

05

Planting

Soft landscaping with proper soil prep, the right species for the site, and mulch on the day. We grow gardens, not put plants in the ground.

06

Irrigation & Drainage

Drip lines, pop-up sprays, smart controllers. Sub-soil drainage, soakage, detention tanks for clay sites.

07

Lighting

Low-voltage garden lighting, path lighting, feature uplights. Designed and installed alongside the build, not bolted on after.

08

Lawns

Ready lawn turf, hydroseed, fine seed lawns. Properly prepared base for a result that takes evenly.

09

Earthworks

Levelling, contouring, bulk excavation, machine work. We have the gear and the operators.

How a build usually runs.

From signed quote to handover. Most residential builds run 4 to 12 weeks on site depending on scope.

01

03

Services

04

The design process.

Five stages from first conversation to construction-ready plans. Each one signed off before the next starts.

01

Discovery

Free site visit. We talk through what is and isn't working, how you want to use the space, what you love and what you cannot stand. No quote yet, no pressure.

02

Site Survey

Measurements, levels, existing planting audit, sun and shade mapping, drainage notes. The information that turns a vibe into a buildable plan.

03

Concept Design

Two concept directions presented for review. You pick one or take ideas from each. We then refine to a single direction.

04

Developed Design

Plant schedule, hardscape detail, 3D visuals where useful, materials specified, build sequence drafted. This is the buildable plan.

05

Build Quote

A detailed construction quote against the design. Take it to us, take it to another builder, do it in stages. Yours to keep.

What good design looks like

Five tests every design has to pass.

  • Will it survive? Plant choices that suit the soil, climate and exposure.
  • Will it be used? Spaces sized and sited around how you actually live.
  • Will it age? Materials and species selected for how they look in year five, not year one.
  • Will it build? Detailed enough that a crew can quote and execute it.
  • Will it maintain? Realistic to keep looking sharp without 20 hours a weekend.
